# M7 (docs/vehicles-companions-design.md section 9 M7, 6.7 and 8.20; docs/native-puppet.md "Seat gun"): shooting as a
# passenger. Part A: the host drives the africa-jeep (veh_lv_0000 = 25608), the guest rides back seat 3 and kills a
# guard while the host drives (reverse). Part B: the roles reversed (the guest drives, the host shoots from seat 3).
# partner_seat_gun=1 (lab key): the driver's copy also holds graph 2 of the seated partner body on GunHoldStart while
# the passenger aims (E9, design A; run 30a6a21a: held, but no visible change) and the passenger sends its seated
# direction (aim_dir=valid; reason=seat).
# Geometry, relative to the jeep wherever it stands. A seated back-seat passenger's body faces the jeep's RIGHT side
# (runs 30a6a21a: body yaw 62.6 deg with the jeep at 152.6 deg; its aim range is +-90 deg around that, so it reaches
# from straight ahead through the right side to straight back), and the aim camera sits behind it: so the passenger
# rides seat 3 and shoots out of the right side (run d40e4126 shot from seat 2 across the jeep: the camera looked
# through the jeep's body; run 30a6a21a from seat 2 out of the left side: the aim clamped ahead). goto_vehicle jeep
# puts a player 1.9 m out on the driver's side facing the jeep; forward 7 carries the host through to 5.1 m out on the
# passenger side, facing away; spawn_enemy here warps a guard 5 m further (10.1 m out; debug_soldier_warp=1, notice
# restricted); enemy sleep nearest puts him to sleep so he does not walk off (a host commit the guest applies). The
# passenger goes to the driver's door and 3.8 m on through the jeep (1.9 m out on the passenger side, facing away) and
# boards: back seat 3, its camera toward the guard. The lever enemy_aim_nearest 0.3 turns its camera to the sleeping
# guard before and during each burst (Player.RequestToSetCameraRotation) while the driver reverses.
# Expected (lab): partner_seat_gun=configured; key=1 on both; the passenger's aim_dir=valid; reason=seat (never
# reason=convention); the driver's copy partner_seat_gun=hold; seq=..; vehicle=25608; seat=3 -> partner_gesture=submit;
# ...; kind=gun_start -> applied; ok=1 -> partner_seat_gun=rates; ok=1 -> release -> partner_gesture=ended; via=end;
# cause=release; null=1. The kill: guest world_lua=event=req; rid=..; ent=.., host world_lua=event=commit; ent=..;
# actor=guest; (Part B: host event=commit; ...; state=3..5; ...; actor=host; guest event=apply; ...; state=3..5).
# Frames (look at them): m7-seat-aim (the passenger's own view), m7-partner-gun (the driver's view of the partner),
# m7-fire-*, m7-after; the same with m7-rev-* for Part B.
