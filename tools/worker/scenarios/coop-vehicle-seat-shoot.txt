# passenger. Part A: the host drives the africa-jeep (veh_lv_0000 = 25608), the guest rides back seat 3 and kills a
# guard while the host drives (reverse). Part B: the roles reversed (the guest drives, the host shoots from seat 3).
# partner_seat_gun=1 (lab key): the driver's copy also holds graph 2 of the seated partner body on GunHoldStart while
# the passenger aims (E9, design A; run 30a6a21a: held, but no visible change) and the passenger sends its seated
# direction (aim_dir=valid; reason=seat).
# Geometry, relative to the jeep wherever it stands. A back-seat passenger's body faces INTO the jeep (runs 30a6a21a /
# 03ceff68: body yaw 62.6 deg in seat 2 and -117.4 deg in seat 3 with the jeep at 152.6 deg), and the stock aim range
# is +-90 deg around it: a passenger shoots across the jeep, never out of its own side (03ceff68: seat 3 aiming right
# clamped). The aim camera sits behind the passenger, so it must stay above the open cabin: seat 3 with the camera
# over its own head (run 6fc3098b's view) aiming left, not seat 2 (run d40e4126: from seat 2 the camera stood outside
# the left side and looked through the jeep's body). goto_vehicle jeep puts a player 1.9 m out on the driver's (left)
# side facing the jeep; forward -12 backs the host to 13.9 m out, still facing the jeep; spawn_enemy here warps a guard
# 5 m in front of it, 8.9 m out on the left (debug_soldier_warp=1, notice restricted); enemy sleep nearest puts him to
# sleep so he does not walk off (a host commit the guest applies). The passenger boards from goto_vehicle jeep right
# (1.9 m out on the right, facing the jeep; facing away boarded nothing in run 6e0edd8b): back seat 3, its camera
# already toward the guard. The lever enemy_aim_nearest 1.6 turns the camera to him (Player.RequestToSetCameraRotation;
# 1.6 m above him because a seated passenger's playerPosY is 1.6 m above the ground: with 0.3 the reticle sat 7.5 deg
# low in run d3409f57) before each single shot (held RMB+LMB bursts climbed to the sky there) while the driver reverses.
# Expected (lab): partner_seat_gun=configured; key=1 on both; the passenger's aim_dir=valid; reason=seat (never
# reason=convention); the driver's copy partner_seat_gun=hold; seq=..; vehicle=25608; seat=3 -> partner_gesture=submit;
# ...; kind=gun_start -> applied; ok=1 -> partner_seat_gun=rates; ok=1 -> release -> partner_gesture=ended; via=end;
# cause=release; null=1. The kill: guest world_lua=event=req; rid=..; ent=.., host world_lua=event=commit; ent=..;
# actor=guest; (Part B: host event=commit; ...; state=3..5; ...; actor=host; guest event=apply; ...; state=3..5).
