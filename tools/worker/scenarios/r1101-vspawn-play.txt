#      (step=pick ... for=guest) and answers kind 3; the guest applies the same locator; frames on both screens.

#      vehicle_seats answers vehicle_not_riding: no ON_VEHICLE in that seat); the host holds the throttle (about 7 m
#      before the concrete block ahead in 5cdbfada); frames on both screens while moving.

#      note 20's toast.
