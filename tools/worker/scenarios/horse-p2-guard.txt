# Horse probe P2 (the horse plan, section 4; lab only; lua/coop/debug_horse.lua): a sleeping stock guard loaded on
# the host's D-Horse by the stock carry and load (no mod carry, no writer), as horse-cargo-npc-baseline.txt. Reads
# IsCarriedHostage / GetCarriedHostage (names by hash: 0xD1233CDA / 0x442D1EEC) before, while loaded, after a dismount and
# after the unload; horse_probe watch logs every 250 ms the horse root, the rider (the host) and the guard in the horse's
# frame (cm forward,right,up) at idle, trot and gallop: the saddle and cargo offsets (its is= field is the read while
# mounted: debug commands are refused then). horse_cargo_native: the cargo slot.

#   pwsh -NoProfile -File tools/worker/Submit-WorkerJob.ps1 -Repo C:/mgs-coop-wt2/horse -Owner horse-probes

#     -WanSim bad -PartnerProfile native -NativeStage gun -BuildProfile research -SaveSeed afgh-ledge -TimeoutSec 600 -Wait
