# Design 8.25 (docs/vehicles-companions-design.md; docs/native-puppet.md "Passenger camera"): the back-seat
# passenger's aim camera. The host drives the africa-jeep (veh_lv_0000 = 25608) and stays still; the guest rides back
# seat 3 (goto_vehicle jeep right: the right side), then back seat 2 (goto_vehicle jeep: the driver's, left side), and
# in each seat aims the way a player does right after boarding: RMB with the camera where boarding left it (across the
# jeep, the seated body's facing), three single LMB shots, release. The lab lever "passcam" picks the guest's passenger
# camera live: aim (the candidate: the handle dropped while aiming plus the shot guard), drop (the handle dropped, no
# guard: the A/B of the guard), connect (0.14.4: the handle for the whole ride). With aim in seat 3, the camera is also
# turned while aiming (mouse) toward the passenger's own side: what the stock aim range (+-90 deg around the seated
# facing) does there.
# Run 4f1a072f (this file's first version) found the shot's pull to the sky with the handle held or dropped alike and
# that the camera's look angles turn vehicle-relative at the aim (so the Lua camera levers aim wrong after it): this
# version turns the camera only with the mouse.
# partner_pass_camera_trace=1 logs the guest's camera every 100 ms while it aims (and 4 s after), else every 1 s.
# Expected (lab): guest partner_ride=pass_camera; state=mode; mode=aim|drop|connect; source=lever; with aim and drop
#   state=aim; event=drop at each aim and event=restore 0.6 s after it ended; with aim state=guard; event=open at the
#   first shot and event=close; writes=..; max_pull_mrad=.. after the last; debug_menu=passcam; ok=1.
# Frames (look at them): s3-seated, s3a-aim / -shot1..3 / -after / -after2, s3a-turn1 / -turn2, s3d-* (drop), then
# seat 2: s2-seated, s2a-*, s2c-* (connect); the host's (driver's) frames at each aim and third shot.
