# The legs start on the open grass 32 m west of the afgh-ledge boot spot, facing east toward the cliff (run 8c0b425f
# faced the ledge at the boot spot and ran into the rock). The guest's hold is the default warp (the stop-AI dog placed
# by LuaAiStayAndSnarl's own warp; run ffb2d7ab compared it with marker, where the dog walks itself and lagged 7-15 m):
# walk (ctrl+w, R4's 3 m), run (w) and dash (shift+w) (5 m). companion_lab mark names the guest's track windows
# (event=companion_track carries band, error, leg, mode and warps).
# away: the guest 40 m from the host (the dog stays with the host). engage (E3, E19): a guard warped beside the host,
# the guest's dog held 1.5 m from the nearest soldier on its copy (lab lever companion_lab near_guard), 55 s calm and
# 65 s in an alert in warp mode, 30 s in marker mode (the active-AI form, for decision D7), then 25 s with the
# guest's protection lever (SetDisableDamage on its dog).
# Post-run checks (log slices): no event=mirror_engaged; event=mirror_near s3 (seconds with a soldier within 3 m of
# the held dog); event=companion_hurt and event=mirror_vars (E19); no result=released; the host's event=obs ...;
# att=buddy kills and their event=commit / the guest's event=apply.
