# Horse probe P1b (the horse plan, section 4, lab only): P1's ride and recording, then the two candidate senders
# (yaw20, sg20) with each viewer warped beside its horse (goto_horse, facing it) before every frame, so the frames show
# the placed horse up close through the whole replay (legs, leaps, heading). play_stats now measure dy and yaw against
# the last placement sent. Session C: -ScenarioPath tools/worker/scenarios/horse-p1b-follow.txt, flags as P1.
# P1 (lua/coop/debug_horse.lua): replay a recorded ride onto the
# unridden D-Horse with SetCallHorse. Both role orders: the host rides and records its own horse (horse_spawn) while the
# guest stands, then the guest rides and records its own (horse_spawn) while the host stands; then both copies replay
# at once (both on foot) in each sender mode, each viewer back at its ride's start (bookmark 1, beside the horse, facing
# it) so the horse crosses the view. Modes: sg4 sg10 sg20 start==goal, yaw20 goal 2 cm ahead (20 Hz), chase2 chase1 from
# where it stands to 3 m ahead of the target (2 / 1 Hz). Lines: world_lua=horse_probe=play_stats; mode=..; err_p50_cm=..;
# leap=..; and every 500 ms horse_probe=play; rt=..; err_cm=..; dy_cm=... Frames p1-<mode>-host / -guest: leap, legs.
