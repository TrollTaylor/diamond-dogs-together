#    preload covered (guns=this_load) or not (guns=next_load).

# loadout yet: no manifest), so the host's allocation refuses no_manifest (source=none); the guest then reaches its
# title, sends its manifest, and the host logs the late_manifest line (guns=next_load: nothing predicted, no handoff).

# takes two sessions, and a second session is a new host process). The fixtures prove it:
