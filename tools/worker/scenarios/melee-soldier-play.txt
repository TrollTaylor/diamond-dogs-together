# other player still does (docs/native-puppet.md "Melee"). Play mode at the SHIPPED defaults: nothing here names the melee
# key (it follows the gun profile), so this also proves the default. Mother Base, mb-ladder seed on both copies,
# -WanSim bad. Punching staff at Mother Base is the common real case.
# The fix (partner_melee Resolve): a strike in reach is held (verdict=hold); PlayerStatus CQC_CONTINUOUS (st= bit 0x100,
# the stock CQC acting on a target) since the press's 113 edge -> verdict=target, no hit; else one land 250 ms after
# the press's 113 ends (its 114 edge).
#   a) the guest about 1 m behind the host, facing it, presses LMB once (no RMB: CQC): guest log verdict=hold, then
#      verdict=land; queued=1; host log friendly_hit=owner (cause 2, down=1). Frames: host ASLEEP on its own screen, the
#      guest sees the host down with HOLD E WAKE UP.
#   f) the guest holds E next to the sleeping host: host downed_status back to down=0 / healthy, both standing.
#   e1) the host goes 1.0 m from the nearest staff member on its copy, facing them (enemy goto nearest 1.0), the guest is
#      placed IN FRONT of the host, to its right (placebehind with a negative distance; about 1.3 m at about 40 degrees
#      after the drop drift), the host goes 1.0 m from the staff member again (staff walk their routes) and presses LMB
#      once: the staff member takes the strike, host log verdict=target; queued=0 with cqc_ms, NO friendly_hit on the
#      guest copy, the guest standing on both screens.
#   e2) the same with the roles swapped (the guest strikes its own copy's nearest staff member, the host ahead-left).
# Each PC moves its own staff (mb-staff-gone-play: the guest's copy of a staff member is elsewhere), so each striker
# aims at the staff on its own copy; the partner is placed by coordinates relative to the striker.
# Expected NEVER (sliced): a friendly_hit=owner line in e1/e2; step=error; viewer_changed; faults; GameOver.
