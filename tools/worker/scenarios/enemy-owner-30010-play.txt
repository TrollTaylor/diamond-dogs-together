# Enemy sync M3 proof (docs/world-sync-design.md 7.9): the guest's own fights show on the host's screen too. Mode play
# over -WanSim bad, soldier_owner named (soldier_follow is the gun profile's default), Afghanistan afgh_field_cp
# (afgh-ledge seed). The outpost goes to ALERT on arrival (every 30010 run since P1b).
#   0. Every afgh_field_cp guard and the LRRP pair ignore the players, the host stands the outpost down (cp_phase nearest
#      sneak: the guards walk, the guest can aim).
#   A. Apart (~70 m: the host by 2961503 at the south-east of the outpost, the guest by 2942862796 at its north): the host
#      gives the guest the guards 15 m nearer it (event=owner; owner=guest; why=near, OWNER 73), the guest streams them
#      (ENEMY_PLACE 71) and the host moves its own copies (event=hplace; WORLD_WARP_HOST_V1), 60 s.
#   B. The guest shoots 2942862796 from 5 m (its own guard): placed on the host first (event=place_first;step=tx;side=guest,
#      then the host's hplace) before the host commits it (actor=guest).
#   C. ALERT then calm-down (cp_phase nearest alert, 10 s, sneak): PHASE flag 2, the guest's CP comes down
#      (event=phase;...;src=down;applied=1) unless its own fight raised it in the last 5 s.
#   D. They meet (the guest 4 m behind the host): ownership goes back to the host (owner=host).
# Run (hidden worker, under the queue):
#   pwsh -File tools/worker/Submit-WorkerJob.ps1 -Owner enemy-sync -ScenarioPath tools/worker/scenarios/enemy-owner-30010-play.txt
#        -PartnerProfile native -NativeStage gun -BuildProfile research -SaveSeed afgh-ledge -Mode play -WanSim bad -Wait
# Then: python tools/enemy_div_report.py --host <host slice> --guest <guest slice> (the M3 lines).
# NEVER: native=role, soldier_warp=disabled, step=error, event=error, GameOver, roster_mismatch, overflow.
