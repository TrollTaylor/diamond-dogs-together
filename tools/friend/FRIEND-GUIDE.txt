MGS CO-OP - PLAY GUIDE
Version @VERSION@ (development build)
==============================================================================

WHAT THIS IS
------------
An unfinished two-player co-op mod for METAL GEAR SOLID V: THE PHANTOM PAIN.
Two people, each on their own PC, play OFFLINE at the same time and each sees
the other player as a second character in Mother Base or Afghanistan/Africa
free roam.

What works today:
- You see your partner's body move, walk and run, crouch and go prone, with
  their own character, face and camouflage in those supported scenes.
- The default (World 3) shares partner movement plus supported enemy
  outcomes, alert, clock/weather, host travel,
  Continue/restart alignment, checkpoints and death (see section 3).
- F9 opens the in-game debug menu. It provides teleport bookmarks, ammo
  controls, equipped-item pickups, existing-guard controls, host time/weather
  and partner movement diagnostics. F9 closes it and restores game controls.
  Actions require supported on-foot gameplay. Guard actions use authored
  mission soldiers; arbitrary new soldiers and unloaded guns are unsupported.
- Gun injuries: a wounded player crawls slowly and cannot aim or fire.
  Friendly sleep darts instead preserve health and use the game's own player
  sleep, lying rest and recovery animations. A healthy teammate can wake or
  revive: close F9, stand within 3 m and HOLD ACTION (E by default) for 3 seconds.
  Both games must be paired in World 3. Allow the native recovery to finish.
  A short co-op prompt shows readiness and actual hold progress.
- Pick up a wounded or sleeping teammate with the normal CARRY hold
  (R by default) beside them; the game's pickup icon remains visible.
  Walk normally while carrying, and hold CARRY again to put them down.
  Native pickup, walking, put-down and manual rescue passed hidden two-copy
  checks. Horse loading, actual shared riding and guard-style upright sleep
  remain unfinished. The offset-position debug mount prototype is off.
  After 30 seconds of fresh gameplay, a sleeping teammate wakes. If carried,
  both games put them down first, finish native recovery and restore controls.
  This passed hidden two-copy checks; fresh two-PC Steam testing is pending.
- F9 > Partner / Co-op includes Incapacitation status, Revive input status and Downed movement input to help
  check pairing, distance and held ACTION input. Close the menu before trying
  the revive. Hidden tests measured about half-speed local crawl and normal
  movement after revival. The hospital opening's exact animation is unavailable.
- F9 > Partner / Co-op > Summon / recall D-Horse requests the local stock horse.
  Wait for loading and check Horse status. Close F9 and use normal controls
  to mount when the horse is present. This does not spawn a second horse,
  attach the remote player, or arm a Walker. When the census realizes a horse
  or Walker body, that body's position and heading are sent to the other
  game. A Walker is moved with SetPosition. A horse is moved with SetCallHorse;
  its goal is one metre along the census heading, which is how that command
  writes facing. The vehicle you are driving is moved with its own SetPosition.
  Parked vehicles stay put. The remote rider is still only the existing
  ride-kind display. A completed revive asks the downed player to stand with
  the game's own stance request. Teammate pickup uses the normal CARRY hold.
- D-Dog follow status reports the shared dog position. The guest dog follows
  the host dog's position; dog attacks, spotting and health are not shared.
- The NATIVE PARTNER (default): your partner's body is moved by the game's
  own movement, aiming, reloading, weapon switching and turning, driven by
  the other player's controls, so it moves like a player and its gun points
  where the other player aims. It carries the other player's three weapons
  (or a gun of the same kind that your game already has loaded). Its shots
  are visual only (muzzle flash and sound, never bullets: each PC counts its
  own player's hits). Riding, unsafe airborne/dead state and stale ride status
  hide the partner until fresh on-foot samples settle; other native failures
  can use the animation-clip fallback (section 3, "PARTNER BODY").
What is NOT fully shared yet:
- Aiming, reloading and weapon switching on the partner body are new in this
  version. If the exact gun is unavailable, the partner can use a loaded
  substitute; the status line identifies it. If no safe gun is available,
  firing and reloading stay off. Prone aim still uses a camera-based
  approximation. Grenade and rocket launchers are
  never shown firing. Debug pickups are local.
- Story missions, items and full campaign progression. This is a development
  build. The user reported partial successful two-PC play on 0.12.0. These
  0.12.6 changes are checked on two hidden local copies and still need a fresh
  two-PC session; complete campaign progression remains unsupported.

One player HOSTS, the other JOINS. Both use this same package (the same
version on both PCs). Konami's servers are never contacted: the game plays
offline, and the message "Failed to log in" at the start is expected.

Two ways to connect:
- ONLINE through Steam (recommended): no port forwarding, no VPN and no extra
  program. Steam connects the two games directly or through Valve's relay
  servers. Send a code, or invite a Steam friend from the game. Partial
  two-PC play worked on 0.12.0; both players need this version for the repairs.
- LAN/VPN: the two games talk over UDP on the same network or through a VPN
  such as Tailscale (sections 4 and 5).


1. WHAT YOU BOTH NEED
---------------------
- MGSV: The Phantom Pain from Steam, version 1.0.15.4, with no other mods (no
  other dinput8.dll, no Infinite Heaven/SnakeBite changes). The installer
  checks the game files and stops if they are not the original ones.
- Saves that can "Continue" into the same supported scene: Mother Base,
  Afghanistan/Africa free roam, or the corresponding helicopter. The easiest
  way: one player shares their save with Share-Save.bat (section 3, SAME
  PROGRESS FOR BOTH). Each installer copies its own player's save.
- Steam running and logged in with your own account.
- The file mgs-coop-player-runtime.dat. It is not in this ZIP; read
  RUNTIME-ARCHIVE.txt to see where it comes from.
- Online play: nothing else (both PCs online in Steam). LAN/VPN play: a way
  for the joining PC to reach the host's PC (section 4); a VPN such as
  Tailscale is the easiest.
- Voice or text chat with each other (Discord, Steam chat...) to send the
  join code (online play can also use a Steam invite between friends).
- Windows 10 or 11. Nothing else needs to be installed.


2. INSTALL (both players, once, about 2 minutes)
------------------------------------------------
 1. Quit MGSV.
 2. Right-click the ZIP > "Extract All..." into a normal folder (for example
    your Desktop). Do not run anything from inside the ZIP.
 3. Double-click Install-Coop.bat.
    (If Windows warns about the file: "More info" > "Run anyway".)
      - Confirm the game folder it found. If it asks for it: in Steam,
        right-click the game > Manage > Browse local files, and copy that path.
      - Say whether you usually host (1) or join (2). This only picks your
        default character; you choose Host or Join again for every game.
      - Character: press Enter for the default (host: your normal character,
        joiner: Quiet), or pick one.
      - If it asks for mgs-coop-player-runtime.dat, drag the file into the
        window and press Enter. (Tip: put the file next to Install-Coop.bat
        and the installer finds it by itself.)
      - Press Enter (or Y) to install.

What the installer does: it puts dinput8.dll and a "mgs-coop" folder into the
game folder (whatever was there before is backed up), backs up your real save
files, and makes a COPY of your save for co-op games. After the install the
mod is inactive: starting MGSV normally from Steam plays exactly as before, on
your real saves. Co-op is switched on only for a game started with
Play-Coop.bat. Run Install-Coop.bat again at any time to change the character.
It also cleans up an earlier test version of this package (its old settings
are renamed *.online-old-<time> and not used again).


3. PLAY (every time)
--------------------
IN SHORT (online):
 - Before the first game: if your saves are at different points of the
   story, share one with Share-Save.bat (SAME PROGRESS FOR BOTH below).
 - Host: Play-Coop.bat, press O, send the code it shows (or invite your
   friend in the game: F9 > Partner / Co-op > Online > Invite a friend).
 - Friend: start Play-Coop.bat FIRST, press J, paste the code (or press
   Enter and accept the host's Steam invite once the game runs).
 - Both: Continue into the same scene (Mother Base or free roam). Your
   partner is shown with the native partner; "Play-Coop.bat -Partner clips"
   uses the animation clips instead (see PARTNER BODY below).
 - Steam does not connect? Use [L] LAN/VPN instead (below).

Play-Coop.bat asks how you want to play:
    [O] Host online (Steam, recommended)
    [J] Join online
    [L] LAN/VPN (UDP code)
    [T] Local two-window test
    [Q] Quit

LOCAL TWO-WINDOW TEST (one PC):
 1. Double-click Play-Coop.bat and press T. Play-Offline-Coop.bat is a
    shortcut to the same option and is included in every friend package.
 2. Use your installed host game and an existing second copy of MGSV. The
    launcher first checks the sibling folder named <host folder>_guest; if
    it cannot find one, paste the guest game folder when asked. You can also
    pass it explicitly: Play-Coop.bat -LocalTest -GuestDirectory "D:\MGSV_guest"
    Both copies must have this package's owned loader and runtime archive.
    The launcher does not download or create a second game installation.
 3. The host opens on the left and the guest on the right. Nothing presses
    startup buttons, Continue, loading-tip keys or travel controls for you.
    Click through each game's screens yourself, choose Continue in BOTH and
    enter the same supported scene, as two players would on separate PCs.
 4. Keyboard and mouse control the game window you click. A controller in
    slot 0 controls the guest. The host ignores controllers so the same
    controller does not move both players. You can still click the guest
    and use keyboard/mouse for its menus and movement.
 5. In the launcher, [R] re-arranges the game windows side by side if the
    game or a display change moves them. Use normal game menus for travel;
    finish each game's loading screen yourself. The launcher shows the
    status of both games and never picks missions or teleports you.
 6. Quit both games normally when finished. The launcher restores each
    copy's previous settings after both games close. Do not close the
    launcher until cleanup finishes.

Local testing uses mgs-coop\local-test-saves in each game folder. On the
first run both are seeded from the host's existing co-op save copy; later
runs keep their own local-test progress. Your REAL saves and ordinary
mgs-coop\coop-saves are unchanged. Windowed graphics apply only to these
local-test save copies. This tests gameplay over local UDP; an online Steam
connection with your friend still needs two PCs.

HOST ONLINE:
 1. Double-click Play-Coop.bat and press O. Steam must be running and logged
    in (online).
 2. It shows your JOIN CODE, for example
        MGS2-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XX@<your Steam account number>
    and copies it to the clipboard. Send it to your friend over chat, or
    invite your friend from the game (step 4). Keep the code private: anyone
    with it can connect while your game runs. A new code is made every time.
 3. Play-Coop starts MGSV through Steam. There are no router, VPN or
    firewall questions.
 4. To invite a Steam friend instead: in the game press F9 > Partner / Co-op
    > Online > "Invite a friend" > Invite. Friends who play MGSV are listed
    first. "Accept joins" there switches new joins on and off.
JOIN ONLINE:
 1. Start Play-Coop FIRST: double-click Play-Coop.bat and press J.
 2. Paste the host's MGS2 code and press Enter ("Code OK" means it is fine),
    OR press Enter without a code to wait for the host's Steam invite.
 3. Play-Coop starts MGSV through Steam. If you are waiting: when the host's
    invite arrives, accept it in the Steam overlay (Shift+Tab) or in the
    Steam chat WHILE the co-op game runs. You can also paste a code later in
    F9 > Partner / Co-op > Online.
    IMPORTANT: do not accept an invite while MGSV is not running from
    Play-Coop. Steam would then start plain MGSV on your REAL saves (the mod
    stays inactive). Quit that game and start Play-Coop first.
 - Invites work between Steam friends; a code works between anyone.
 - An MGS1 code pasted here joins over LAN/VPN instead.

HOST (LAN/VPN):
 1. Double-click Play-Coop.bat, press L, then H.
 2. It shows one JOIN CODE per network address of your PC, for example:
        same network (LAN)        MGS1-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XX@192.168.1.20:47830
        Tailscale VPN             MGS1-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XX@100.x.y.z:47830
    and copies the one you choose to the clipboard. Send that code to your
    friend over chat. Keep it private: anyone with the code can connect while
    your game runs. A new code is made every time you host.
 3. Answer the optional questions (each is "No" unless you type y):
      [P] look up your public IP address (contacts api.ipify.org),
      [U] ask your router to forward UDP 47830 to your PC (UPnP),
      [F] add a Windows Firewall rule for MGSV (administrator prompt).
 4. Play-Coop starts MGSV through Steam. Answer any Steam dialog.
JOINER (LAN/VPN):
 1. Double-click Play-Coop.bat, press L, then J.
 2. Paste the host's code (right-click pastes in the window) and press Enter.
    "Code OK: host ...:47830" means it is fine. A typo is reported, for
    example "The code has a typo; copy it again."
 3. Play-Coop starts MGSV through Steam.
BOTH:
 - Click through the start screens ("Failed to log in" is normal), choose
   Continue and meet in Mother Base or Afghanistan/Africa free roam.
   The partner body supports missions 30050, 30010 and 30020. In free roam,
   stay near one another; a distant partner is hidden until you meet again.
 - Leave the Play-Coop window open. Every second it shows a status line:
        [HOST] link=connected rtt=42ms jitter=3ms loss=0.4% interp=80ms | partner=live shown look | mission 30050
   link=connected means the two PCs talk to each other; partner=live shown
   means your partner is on screen. Online games add "via Steam (relay)" or
   "via Steam (direct)"; a joiner waiting for an invite shows link=waiting.
   An online game with the native partner on both PCs looks like this:
        [JOIN] link=connected via Steam (relay) rtt=61ms jitter=4ms loss=0.0% interp=100ms | partner=live shown look native; you on the other PC: native | mission 30010
 - In the game, a "CO-OP [...]" line at the top right shows the same link
   (section 6).
 - Quit MGSV normally when you are done. Co-op switches itself off when the
   game exits, even if the Play-Coop window was closed: the next normal start
   from Steam is plain MGSV on your real saves again.

PARTNER BODY (native by default):
 - Both PCs use the native partner unless a player chooses the clips. It
   follows the other player's movement, aim, reloads and weapon switches.
   Riding, unsafe airborne/dead state and stale ride status hide the partner
   until fresh on-foot movement settles.
 - The status line shows how YOUR game shows your partner and how the other
   game shows you, for example
        partner=live shown look native; you on the other PC: native
   "clips (...)" names why a game uses the animation clips right now, e.g.
   "the other player is paused or loading" or "the connection dropped": it
   goes back to native by itself. "this PC runs the game's update threads
   differently" or "too slow on this PC" stay on clips: send the logs
   (section 8).
 - To always use the clips (for example on a slow PC), open Command Prompt
   in the extracted package folder and start Play-Coop with
        Play-Coop.bat -Partner clips
   then choose O, J or L as usual. Only your game switches to the clips; the
   other player can still use the native partner for your body.

SAME PROGRESS FOR BOTH (Share-Save; do this before your first game):
 - Co-op only pairs when both games are in the same Mother Base, free roam
   or helicopter. A save at the very start of the story (just after the
   hospital) always Continues into a story mission that co-op cannot join.
 - The player whose save already reaches Mother Base and free roam: quit
   MGSV, double-click Share-Save.bat and press E. It saves
   mgs-coop-save-<time>.zip on the Desktop. Send that file to the other
   player.
 - The other player: quit MGSV, double-click Share-Save.bat, press I and
   drag the zip into the window. Your own co-op copy is kept aside, and
   Share-Save.bat [R] brings it back. Your real saves are never touched.
 - Keep the zip. If Install-Coop.bat later asks to replace your co-op save
   copy, answer N; if it was replaced anyway, import the zip again.
 - MGSV saves may be tied to the Steam account. If the game does not accept
   the imported save (an error, or a new game at the title), run
   Share-Save.bat and press R.

EXPERIMENTAL SHARED WORLD (enabled by default):
 - A normal double-click enables World 3. To choose the level explicitly,
   open Command Prompt in the extracted package folder on BOTH PCs:
        Play-Coop.bat -World 3
   Then choose O (or L, H) on the host and J (or L, J) on the joining PC.
 - Both games must first enter the same supported scene to pair. Different
   saved scenes do not bootstrap automatically. Once paired, the host chooses
   travel, Continue/restart and abort; the guest follows. Press Space on each
   game's loading-tip screen when asked.
 - The host's helicopter waits up to 2 minutes for a partner who can come
   and says so on screen; choose the deployment again to fly alone. With no
   partner connected, or the partner in another mission, it leaves at once.
 - Enemy sync waits for a matching readable host snapshot after reload;
   unloaded areas may delay it. Existing terminal guest enemies are never
   revived. Story missions and full campaign progression remain unsupported.
 - For partner movement only, use Play-Coop.bat -World 0 on both PCs. The
   launcher keeps each existing co-op save copy between sessions.


4. HOW THE JOINER REACHES THE HOST
----------------------------------
ONLINE: nothing to set up. Steam finds a direct path between the two PCs or
carries the traffic through Valve's relay servers (no port forwarding, VPN or
router setting; Konami's servers are not involved). Steam's own setting
"Allow direct connections (IP sharing)" decides whether a direct path may be
used; with it off, everything goes through the relays.

LAN/VPN: the joiner's game sends small UDP packets to the host's PC, port
UDP 47830. Pick ONE way:
 a) A VPN on both PCs (recommended, no router settings): Tailscale
    (tailscale.com), ZeroTier or Radmin VPN. Both join the same VPN network;
    the host sends the code labelled with the VPN.
 b) Port forwarding: in the host's router, forward UDP 47830 to the host PC's
    LAN address (the LAN code shows it). Then the host sends the "internet"
    code ([P] or [U] finds the public address). Some internet connections
    (CGNAT, DS-Lite) cannot be reached this way at all; use a VPN then.
 c) Both PCs in the same home network: use the LAN code.
The joiner's own router needs no settings.


5. WINDOWS FIREWALL
-------------------
ONLINE: Windows may ask once whether MGSV may use the network. Allow is
better (a direct connection becomes possible); Cancel still connects through
Steam's relays.

LAN/VPN: the first time MGSV opens the co-op port, Windows asks whether MGSV may use
the network. Choose ALLOW, and tick "Public networks" too if your network is
set to Public (Install-Coop and Play-Coop show your network type). The
"Allow" button alone covers Private networks only.
If the prompt was closed or refused, Windows created a BLOCK rule for MGSV,
and Block rules win over Allow rules. Fix: Play-Coop, Host, [F]: it lists the
Block rules, offers to remove them and adds an Allow rule for UDP 47830
(administrator prompt). Uninstall-Coop.bat offers to remove that rule again.


6. IF IT DOES NOT WORK
----------------------
In the game, a "CO-OP [...]" line at the top right shows the link (for
example "CO-OP [connected] <friend> - Steam relay 61 ms") and short messages
appear below it when something changes. To hide it, start Play-Coop.bat
from a command prompt in this folder as: Play-Coop.bat -Hud off
(-Hud full shows more detail).
The Play-Coop status line explains most problems:
- link=connecting for a long time: the joiner cannot reach the host. Online:
  are both PCs online in Steam, is the host's game running and "Accept joins"
  on, is it the right code? LAN/VPN: check section 4 (VPN connected on both
  PCs? port forwarded? right code?) and the host's firewall (section 5).
- "Steam is offline or not logged in": start Steam, log in, start Play-Coop
  again.
- "Steam networking changed": a Steam update changed what the mod checks.
  Use LAN/VPN ([L]) for now and send the logs (section 8).
- "Possibly a wrong code": copy the code again; the host may have started
  Play-Coop again (new code).
- "The host is already connected to someone": only one joiner at a time.
- "The host's game did not answer": the host's game is not running from
  Play-Coop (Host online), "Accept joins" is off, or the code is old.
- "your friend left" / "the host left": the other player quit the game;
  they can join again with the same code while your game keeps running.
- "Your friend runs mgs-coop X; you run Y": both must install the same
  package version.
- "Different missions never pair" (Play-Coop) or "partner in ..." (in game):
  your partner is in another mission. Meet in the same Mother Base, free
  roam or helicopter. If one game always Continues into a story mission,
  share a save (section 3, SAME PROGRESS FOR BOTH).
- "Partner body unavailable in this mission": you are in a story mission;
  meet in Mother Base or free roam.
- "Your co-op save is at the story start": see section 3, SAME PROGRESS FOR
  BOTH.
- "the host restarted with a new code" / "[the host has a new code]": paste
  the host's new code in F9 > Partner / Co-op > Online; your game can keep
  running.
- "That code was not used": you are already connected or connecting.
- "game not responding": the game hangs or is in a loading screen for a long
  time; wait, or restart both games.
- A RED banner "Co-op did NOT start and save protection is NOT active" means
  that this game is normal offline play on your REAL saves. Quit MGSV if you
  do not want that, and send the logs (section 8).
- The partner stands still or disappears: check the link and partner status.
  Riding, unsafe airborne/dead state or stale status can hide it until the
  other player is back on foot and fresh movement settles.
- "partner=... clips (...)": see section 3, PARTNER BODY.
- Something else, a crash or a freeze: note the time and what you did, and
  send the logs.


7. YOUR SAVES
-------------
- Co-op games play from a COPY of your save, made at install. Your real save
  files are backed up and are not written during co-op games.
- Normal starts from Steam (without Play-Coop) use your REAL saves, with the
  mod loaded but inactive. Progress does NOT carry over between the two: what
  you do in co-op stays in the copy, and the copy does not see later normal
  progress.
- Share-Save.bat (section 3) copies progress between the two players' co-op
  copies only. Install-Coop.bat asks twice before it replaces a co-op copy
  that holds your partner's imported progress: answer N to keep it.
- When you stop playing co-op, run Uninstall-Coop.bat (section 9).


8. COLLECT AND SEND THE LOGS (both players)
-------------------------------------------
 1. Quit MGSV (collecting while it runs also works, but quitting is better).
 2. Double-click Collect-Logs.bat.
 3. It saves mgs-coop-logs-host-<time>.zip (or -join-) on your Desktop.
    Send that file to the person who gave you this package.
The ZIP contains only the mod's own log and settings files from the
"mgs-coop" folder (the script log covers your last 3 game starts), a list
of the co-op save folders (file names, sizes and times only, never save
contents) and a summary of file versions and save checks. Join codes (MGS1
and MGS2), invite strings, the link key and public IP addresses are not in
it (they are removed or masked), SteamIDs
are masked except their last 4 digits, and your Windows user name is removed
from paths. You can open the ZIP to check what is in it.


9. UNINSTALL
------------
 1. Quit MGSV.
 2. Double-click Uninstall-Coop.bat (in the same extracted folder), press
    Enter (or Y).
It switches off a co-op session that did not end cleanly, puts back what was
in the game folder before (it removes dinput8.dll if there was none), offers
to remove the firewall rule Play-Coop added, and checks that your real save
files are unchanged since the backup made at install ("Real save files:
UNCHANGED"). It never overwrites your saves; if it reports a difference (for
example because you played normally in between), it prints which two folders
to copy back from that backup. The backups, the co-op save copy and the logs
stay in <game folder>\mgs-coop; you can delete that folder yourself later.


10. KNOWN LIMITS AND RISKS - PLEASE READ
----------------------------------------
- Development build. Partner bodies support Mother Base and Afghanistan/
  Africa free roam. World 3 is experimental and enabled by default;
  story missions, items and full campaign progression are not supported.
  Downed: crawl slowly; aiming and shooting are blocked. A teammate revives
  you by holding ACTION (E by default) within 3 m for 3 seconds. Close F9
  while holding ACTION. F9 > Partner / Co-op has Downed status and Revive
  input status, plus read-only carry/sleep diagnostics and horse controls. Wait for the
  summoned local horse, close F9, then mount with normal game controls.
  Loading a teammate onto a horse, shared horse/vehicle
  passengers and two shared rideable horses/Walkers remain unfinished. Sleep
  is immobile, keeps health, and wakes after 30 seconds or a teammate E hold.
  The crawl uses
  the normal prone animation at reduced speed, not the exact hospital clip.

  Partial two-PC Steam play was reported on 0.12.0. This 0.12.6 build needs
  a fresh two-PC test; its recorded qualification uses two hidden instances.
- Online play uses Steam's networking and Valve's relays: it needs both PCs
  online in Steam. If it does not connect, use LAN/VPN.
- LAN/VPN play talks directly over UDP without a relay: if neither a VPN nor
  port forwarding works for you, use online play.
- A Steam invite carries the join code; Steam keeps it in your chat history.
  Each code works only for the game session it was made for.
- The join code is a key: whoever has it can connect while the host's game
  runs. Send it only to your friend. Windows clipboard history (Win+V) may
  keep a copy; Play-Coop clears the clipboard when the game exits, but not
  the history.
- The mod and the installer never change mgsvtpp.exe, steam_api64.dll or
  Steam itself, never contact Konami's servers, and change firewall or router
  settings only when you answer "y" to [F] or [U].
