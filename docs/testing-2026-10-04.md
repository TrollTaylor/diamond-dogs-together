# October 4 live run: offline partner spike (0.11.0, coop-lab ae047e5)

These were the first live runs of fully offline co-op using our own netcode. The
setup was two MGSV instances on one PC (`MGS_TPP` host, `MGS_TPP_guest`
guest) in the `offline-v1` marker mode:
- the master URL pointed at a closed local port, so there was no server and no login;
- no fuse;
- no game session; `S_IS_ONLINE` was never set and the synchronizer was never touched;
- our own UDP link on 47820/47821.

The experiments follow [the spike plan](offline-partner-spike-plan.md), section 3. The
verdicts come from `tools/lab/Test-PartnerSpike.ps1`, plus screenshots.

## Results

| Exp | What | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| E0 | Offline boot, partner config, our link, units | **PASS** (both) | `mode=offline_v1; sessions=0; s_is_online=never`. `partner_cfg=ready; count=2; realized_count=2; local=0; R=1; online_bit=0; sync=null; code_gate=ok`. The link was live both ways (about 5,300 frames each). Lua and memory positions agreed exactly. The tick ran at 59 Hz in the unfocused window too. |
| E1 | Lua `Warp` reaches instance 1 | **PASS** | `partner_warp_check ... err_mm=0`, with ids[1] still `ffff` |
| E2 | `SetAllInstanceActiveMode(true)` alone | **PASS, outcome B** | `partner_realized=1; R=1; id=0001; pool=01`. Pure-Lua realization works; the native realize bits are not needed. |
| E3 | Copy parts, then model load | **PASS** | `partner_parts=loaded; R=1`. A **second Snake was visible** 3 m in front of the player (`partner-spike-2026-10-04/e3-host-100323.png`). It ran 120 s+ with no `crash_probe=av`. |
| E4 | Orbit with no network | **PASS** | The partner circled the player in a walking pose (`e4-grid.png`), with `err_mm=0` every second. |
| E6 | Driven by the other instance's packets | **PASS** (both directions) | The guest walked 4.8 m, and the host's partner stood at the guest's exact position (`e6-grid.png`). The host walked a circle, and the guest's partner slot matched the host's own position `8283,800,-42500`. Link status `live`, `last_age_ms=0`. |
| E8 | Stop, re-realize, quit while active | **PASS** | `allactive_cleared` was followed by `partner_realized=0`. `stage=3` brought it back with the parts reloaded in 15 ms. Both games quit cleanly with 0 access violations. |

The real saves were `UNCHANGED` after the run. The lab config was disabled afterwards.

## What this establishes

Each instance can show the other player as a real, game-native player body,
offline. It uses only the game's local player functions (all-active mode, parts
copy, Warp), and the position comes from our own network link. No official server,
login or game session is involved.

## Limits and next steps

- **Movement is position warps at about 30 Hz.** The partner holds one pose and
  does not play the walking, running or crouching animation of the real player.
  Next: drive the motion and stance state (plan item 2c and the E10 animation
  survey).
- **The partner looks like the local player's appearance** (parts copied from
  local). Next: send each player's character (for example the guest as Quiet) and
  apply it with `SetPartsInfoAtInstanceIndex`.
- **Only free-roam Mother Base (30050) was tested.** Missions, enemies, combat
  and shared world state are not synced yet.
- **The single-PC link is UDP on 127.0.0.1.** Two PCs need a transport over the
  internet: Steam P2P on our own channel, or direct UDP.
